```
Family-friendly 3D animated film still, Pixar-Disney style, warm pastel palette,
soft cinematic lighting, extremely expressive cartoon faces, highly detailed
soft textures, shallow depth of field, 16:9 widescreen, no text, no watermark.
```

## المفتاح الأساسي (Seed Prompts)

### ١. تأسيس البيت — `s01_livingroom`
> 3D animated family film still, Pixar-Disney style, wide cinematic establishing shot,
> cozy Arabian living room flooded with warm golden afternoon sun through sheer curtains.
> A kind Arab father (35, short black hair, light beard, beige linen home shirt) kneels
> assembling a small light-wood baby crib. A 5-year-old girl with double ponytails and a
> yellow dress holds a plastic measuring tape upside-down. A 3-year-old with chubby cheeks
> in colorful pajamas places a small brown teddy bear into the crib. An 8-year-old with two
> dark braids and a navy cardigan carries a folded blanket. Warm rug, cushions, wooden
> coffee table, potted plant, framed photos, toys. Volumetric light rays, dust motes.

### ٢. جيجي وخالد — `s02_gigi_dad`
> Medium close-up. The father crouches to eye level with a delighted laughing face; the
> 3-year-old holds her teddy up in both arms, wide curious sparkling eyes. Softly out of
> focus behind them: a small wooden crib. Warm cream and honey tones.

### ٣. قلق نانا — `s03_nana_worried`
> Emotional medium shot. The 8-year-old with two braids and navy cardigan hugs a small
> folded baby blanket against her chest, standing hesitantly in a doorway, big brown eyes
> worried and thoughtful, biting her lower lip. Crib softly blurred behind her.

### ٤. حوار الشموع — `s04_candle_talk`
> Two-shot. The father holds both of his daughter's hands, his other hand pressed on his
> own chest over his heart, gentle reassuring smile; the girl's eyes shine with relief. In
> warm bokeh behind: three lit candles on a wooden side table, star-shaped lanterns.

### ٥. فريق الحراسة — `s05_mission_cheer`
> Three happy sisters celebrate together wearing matching handmade white paper hats with
> stars, hands raised in a joyful cheer; the father smiles proudly behind them, leaning on
> the crib. Paper stars in the air, warm pastel tones.

### ٦. العناق الجماعي — `s06_group_hug`
> Emotional wide shot: the father kneels on a patterned rug wrapping his three daughters
> in a big warm hug, all smiling with eyes closed, foreheads touching, paper star hats.

### ٧. الهاتف — `s07_phone_rings`
> Extreme close-up insert: a smartphone vibrating on a wooden coffee table, screen glowing
> soft blue, blurred colorful toys and a folded baby blanket around, warm bokeh, tense but
> cozy mood.

### ٨. ورشة الهدية — `s08_workshop_table`
> Top-down overhead of a cozy wooden crafting table: colored pencils, pastel paper cut into
> stars and smiling clouds with raindrops, safety scissors, glue stick, a hand-painted card,
> and three painted handprints (rainbow, medium, tiny) side by side.

### ٩. سوسو ترسم — `s09_susu_drawing`
> Cheerful close-up: the 5-year-old with double ponytails leans over the table drawing a big
> blue smiling cloud with an orange pencil, tongue slightly out, proud cheeky smile.

### ١٠. نانا تكتب — `s10_nana_card`
> Warm close-up: the 8-year-old carefully writes on a pastel greeting card, focused and
> gentle, small smile; paper stars, glue stick, colored pencils around.

### ١١. بصمة جيجي — `s11_gigi_handprint`
> Delightful close-up: the 3-year-old, hands covered in rainbow finger paint, presses her
> small hand onto a pastel poster already showing two larger handprints, paint smudges on
> her cheeks, teddy bear beside her.

### ١٢. خالد جاهز — `s12_dad_ready`
> The father in a beige coat carrying a small overnight bag, smiling proudly down at his
> three daughters in paper star hats; the eldest holds a rolled poster, the middle bounces
> with excitement, the youngest hugs her teddy.

### ١٣. مسيرة البطلات — `s13_hero_walk`
> Playful low-angle hero shot from behind: three sisters walk in a confident line toward a
> bright doorway, holding hands, paper star hats; warm golden light floods the doorway.

### ١٤. ممر المستشفى — `s14_corridor`
> Gentle wide shot of a bright hospital corridor, pale mint walls, light wood, polished
> reflective floor. The three sisters tiptoe forward holding hands, hushed and curious.

### ١٥. أحضان الأم — `s15_mother_arms`
> Tender hospital scene: the 32-year-old mother in a blush robe sits up in bed with a tired
> happy smile, arms open wide as her daughters rush to hug her gently; monitor glowing
> softly in bokeh.

### ١٦. سرير غيث — `s16_bassinet`
> A clear plastic hospital bassinet with a newborn boy swaddled in white with light-blue
> embroidered stars, sleeping; two sisters lean in to peek with wide eyes and clasped hands.

### ١٧. وجه غيث — `s17_baby_closeup`
> Emotional close-up: the newborn opens his eyes slowly, rosy cheeks, soft dark hair; his
> tiny curled fist grips his big sister's small gentle finger. Glowing rim light.

### ١٨. دهشة جيجي — `s18_gigi_wow`
> The 3-year-old with one finger on her lips in awe, eyes huge, chubby cheeks, pajamas,
> teddy bear under her arm, leaning toward the bassinet.

### ١٩. إصبع نانا — `s19_nana_pinky`
> Macro-emotional shot: the 8-year-old's fingertip held entirely by the newborn's tiny hand,
> her eyes glistening with a wide smile, tears of joy.

### ٢٠. فخر سارة — `s20_sara_proud`
> The mother looks at her daughters with brimming eyes, hand over her heart, soft warm
> hospital light, cream blanket, monitors blurred.

### ٢١. الأخوات حول السرير — `s21_sisters_around`
> The three sisters lean over the bassinet in matching paper star hats, warm lamplight,
> father and mother embracing behind them.

### ٢٢. اللقطة الواسعة للأنشودة — `s22_song_wide`
> Slight 360° feel: the whole family around the bassinet, warm golden light, paper stars
> floating, comfortable cinematic composition with room for on-screen lyrics.

### ٢٣. الصورة التذكارية — `s24_photo_frame`
> The same family portrait inside a warm wooden frame hanging on a cozy wall, soft vignette,
> nostalgic childhood-photo mood.

## ملاحظات توليد
- استخدم `negative prompt`: `text, watermark, extra fingers, deformed hands, scary, dark,
  extra limbs, low detail, blurry faces`.
- أي لقطة بها أطفال: صف الملابس صراحةً (modest clothing) وتجنّب كلمات مثل
  “bare”/“naked”/“skin” لتفادي حجب المولّد.
- للاتساق: أعد استخدام نفس وصف المظهر حرفيًا لكل شخصية في كل لقطة.
